FAQ

We've got answers.

How long does a typical project take?

Ready products like Workflo go live in about 48 hours. Custom dashboards and automations usually take 1–3 weeks; full web or mobile apps 4–10 weeks depending on scope.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Most of our clients are growing Indian businesses. We start small — often with one dashboard or automation — and scale as you see results.

Can you connect our existing tools?

Yes. We integrate CRMs, accounting software, Google Sheets, WhatsApp, e-commerce platforms and databases so data flows automatically between them.

Is our data safe with you?

We follow strict access controls, encryption and confidentiality. Your data stays yours, and we're happy to sign an NDA.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes handover and training, and we offer ongoing support and improvement plans.

How do we get started?

Send us a message through the form below or the chat. We'll book a free 30-minute call to understand your needs and suggest the best approach.
